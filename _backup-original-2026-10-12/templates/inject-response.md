> Injects are scored client interactions: a surprise request, a scope change, an urgent question, a "can you also look at…". Answer **on time**, **in character**, and **in the client's terms**. Late is worse than short.

## The request, in our words
One sentence restating what the client asked, to confirm we understood.

## Response
The direct answer first, then supporting detail. If the request conflicts with the scope or ROE, say so politely and offer the in-scope alternative (e.g., "That host is outside the agreed scope. We can test it if you confirm in writing.").

## Supporting evidence
Finding IDs, screenshots, or data that back the answer.

## Recommendations / next steps
What the client should do, and what we'll do next.

## Open questions for the client
Anything we need from them.
